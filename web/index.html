Your gateway.
Ready when you are.

Install LVS Gateway firmware directly from your browser. No separate flashing utility is needed—just a supported browser and a USB data connection.

Install firmware

Follow these steps to safely flash your ESP32.

1
Connect your boardUse a USB data cable and connect the ESP32 to this computer.
2
Choose ConnectSelect your board's serial port in the browser dialog.
3
Confirm and installFollow the installer prompts and keep the board connected until it finishes.

The installer requires a Chromium-based browser with Web Serial support, such as Chrome or Edge, and this page must be served over HTTPS.

ⓘ
Having trouble connecting?
Close serial monitors or other flashing tools. If the board will not connect, hold BOOT while the installer starts connecting, then release it.

Before you begin

USB data cable

Some cables provide power only and cannot flash firmware.

Supported board

Use a compatible ESP32 or ESP32-S3 board for the firmware build you intend to install.

Keep power connected

Do not unplug or reset the board while flashing is in progress.

Need the source?

Review the repository for build instructions, firmware code, and project updates.
